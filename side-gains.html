Posture reset

Do it every hour you sit
  • Stack your ears over your shoulders, and your shoulders over your hips.
  • Draw your shoulder blades back and down, as if putting them in your back pockets.
  • Tuck your chin slightly, like making a double chin, and hold for 5 seconds.
  • Raise your screen and your phone to eye level instead of dropping your head.

How scoring works

  • Base points by rarity: Common 100 · Rare 200 · Epic 400 · Legendary 800.
  • Speed bonus: 1st approved finisher +50%, 2nd +25%, 3rd +10%. Order is by submission time.
  • Streak bonus: +10 per consecutive approved day after the first, up to +50.
  • Approval: a majority of the other members must vote it valid. A majority of rejections throws it out.
  • Hours: each quest opens at 12 AM and closes at 10 PM (your local time). No late submissions.
  • Punishments: when the week ends (Sunday midnight), whoever has the fewest weekly points has to do the punishment the crew voted for that week on the Punishment tab. Film it and let the crew judge it by the end of the next week, or lose 100 points.
